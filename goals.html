★ CLUB GOALS ★

(a.k.a. The Cool Club Philosophy)

The rules tell you how to get in. This is why the club exists at all. Four goals. Nothing complicated.

1. HAVE FUN
This is goal number one for a reason. The whole point of the club is to have a good time. If something stops being fun, we change it.
2. BE KIND
Nobody wants a club where people are mean to each other. Be cool to one another, be patient with beginners, and assume good intentions. That is it. That is the rule.
3. MEET NEW PEOPLE
It is always cool to meet new people. Learn about each other’s hobbies, swap stories, and connect with folks from all over the Information Superhighway. Bonsai enjoyers, tuna lovers, rabbit fans, air guitarists and Admirals are all welcome here.
4. KEEP BUILDING
Build new things, and keep building. Websites, tools, protocols, videos, whatever the club dreams up. We learned to code together once. Now let’s make something together.
