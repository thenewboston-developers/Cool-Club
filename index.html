⚠ CLUB RULES ⚠

  1. Be cool.
  2. Be nice to people. Being nice is cool. Being mean is the opposite of cool.
  3. Be yourself. Being unique is cool, and nobody else is qualified to be you.
  4. Create value for the community. Help someone, build something, teach something, share something. This is the coolest thing you can do in the Cool Club.
  5. All web pages must be valid XHTML. Close your tags! (RoterFruchtZwerg has been granted a one-time marquee exemption. His tags are closed. The validator has not been told.)
