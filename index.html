Bonsai Facts for Cool People
  • "Bonsai" literally means "planted in a container." Small tree, big cool.
  • Some bonsai trees are over 800 years old. That is older than the Internet.
  • Bonsai trees are NOT a species. Any tree can be a bonsai if it believes in itself.
  • 100% of Cool Club founders like bonsai trees.
  • A bonsai tree pairs surprisingly well with a tuna sandwich. (Research ongoing.)

Recipe: The Cool Club Tuna-Bacon Melt
  1. Fry bacon until it is crispier than a 28.8k modem handshake.
  2. Mix one can of tuna with mayo. Do not skimp.
  3. Stack tuna, bacon and cheese on bread. Grill until golden.
  4. Eat next to a bonsai tree for maximum coolness (optional, but recommended).
